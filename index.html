New York Academy of Sciences · Junior Academy · Spring 2025

Hydrolink AI

Team of five

A proposal for a Transparent AI Framework that keeps people thinking critically instead of over-relying on AI.

It lets users choose how much of the model's reasoning they see, maps decisions as interactive decision trees, and traces answers back to their data sources.

An ethics and community dashboard flags bias and suggests fixes, while prompt recommendations help everyone use AI equally well, aimed at education, finance and media.
